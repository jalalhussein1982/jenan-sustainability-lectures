## Overview
A calm, practical download library carrying over the lecture decks' navy, green, ivory and serif typography. Native links and clear content take priority over decorative interaction.

## Colors
Green identifies presentation downloads; navy carries headings. Ivory is inherited from the supplied lecture design. Terracotta distinguishes the subject line. Secondary labels retain strong contrast.

## Typography
Georgia for headings and lecture numbers, Arial for operational text, matching the lecture materials. Functional text is at least 12px.

## Layout
A maximum 1180px content area. Desktop presents number, topic and download pair in three columns. Below 650px, files stack beneath the topic. Numbering expresses the actual lecture sequence.

## Elevation & Depth
Flat background and ruled rows. The current anchor receives a subtle background highlight.

## Shapes
Small rounded controls and square-edged row divisions.

## Components
Each lecture has a filled PowerPoint link and outlined PDF guide link. Both show format-specific counts and file sizes. The complete ZIP is available near the top. A guide note explains the PDF purpose before the lecture list.

## Do's and Don'ts
Keep paired files within the same row. Preserve lecture numbers and precise download names. Do not claim a click means a completed download. Avoid repeated slide-cover thumbnails, which would make different lectures look the same.
